# SteamAssist - Requirement Analysis
Steam assistant that takes your games as a database. 
1. It recommends games that are either in your steam account or outside of it depending on what you played recently/played the most.
2. It also lists the games depending on their predicted game length.
3. All recommendation lists can get filters added to them to add restrictions.
4. You must log in or at least add your user ID so that it can access your games.
